Star-chart arrangements

Five selections from ten ideas. Each keeps duration, start, wake-up time, and sleep imagery.

Night passage

Duration centered between bedtime and dawn. 64px

Lunar seal

An oversized crescent beside duration, with the interval below. 64px

Night canopy

Duration above a drawn night horizon; times mark its edges. 88px

Star ledger

A quiet column of times, followed by the duration. 52px

Moon dial

Duration inside a lunar medallion, with the endpoints beneath. 104px

The ten ideas and selection notes
  1. Night passage Selected. Moon/start at left, duration in the center, sunrise/end at right. Symmetrical and quick to scan.
  2. Lunar seal Selected. Large crescent on the left; duration above a compact start-to-end interval. Strong sleep identity.
  3. Night canopy Selected. Duration above a night horizon; start and end anchor the bottom corners. Gives the scene room without obscuring data.
  4. Star ledger Selected. Moon and stacked endpoints on the left, duration on the right. The densest option, keeping the 52px height.
  5. Moon dial Selected. Centered duration inside an engraved lunar ring, endpoints below. A distinctive centered alternative.
  6. Hammock Not selected. Duration rests in a curved hammock with times at its anchors. Curves become cramped at narrow widths.
  7. Moon phases Not selected. Five phases span the top, with duration and endpoints below. Phases imply information the tracker does not record.
  8. Hourglass Not selected. Hours and minutes occupy two chambers with endpoint labels at the sides. Splits the duration and slows reading.
  9. Vertical orbit Not selected. Start, moon/duration, and end follow a vertical orbit. Consumes too much height for three values.
  10. Folded chart Not selected. A folded corner carries the moon; duration and times sit on separate panels. Adds structure without improving scanning.
